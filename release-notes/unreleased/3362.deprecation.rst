class:`~qiskit_ibm_runtime.EstimatorV2` and class:`~qiskit_ibm_runtime.SamplerV2` are deprecated.
Two new client-side class:`~qiskit_ibm_runtime.executor_estimator.Estimator` and client-side
class:`~qiskit_ibm_runtime.executor_sampler.Sampler` primitives provide a new implementation that
runs all the pre- and post-processing in the client side.

Both sets of primitives will co-exist until the deprecation process is completed. The top-level
imports (``qiskit_ibm_runtime.Sampler`` and ``qiskit_ibm_runtime.Estimator``, as well as the aliases
with their ``V2`` suffix) will be updated in an upcoming release to point to the client-side
Primitives in an upcoming, maintaining the legacy primitives available as part of the package in a
different namespace until a later release.
