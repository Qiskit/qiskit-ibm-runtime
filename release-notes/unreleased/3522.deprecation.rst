The entries related to seconds in the dictionary returned by :meth:`.QiskitRuntimeService.usage`
(``usage_consumed_seconds``, ``usage_limit_seconds``, ``usage_allocation_seconds`` and
``usage_limit_seconds``) are now deprecated, and will be removed in a future release. Use their
equivalent resource units keys (``usage_consumed_ru``, ``usage_limit_ru``, ``usage_allocation_ru``
and ``usage_limit_ru``) instead.
